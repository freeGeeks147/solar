Frequently asked questions

What does it cost to charge an EV at home in Gujarat?

Each extra unit is charged at your highest slab plus FPPPA and duty, usually ₹6.5–9 per unit. A compact SUV EV doing 1,000 km a month adds about ₹1,306 to an Ahmedabad home's bill, about ₹1.31 per km.

Is it cheaper to charge during the day?

With a smart meter, units used between 11:00-17:00 get a 60 paise discount. If you have rooftop solar, charging in the day uses your own surplus, which would otherwise earn only about ₹2.25/unit from the DISCOM.

How much does public EV charging cost?

Public DC fast chargers in Gujarat typically charge ₹18–25 per kWh including GST, two to three times the home rate. That is about ₹2.80 per km for a compact SUV.
