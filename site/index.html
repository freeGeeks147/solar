Frequently asked questions

What is the price of solar panels for a home in India in 2026?

A complete on-grid rooftop system costs about ₹60,000–₹80,000 for 1 kW, ₹1.65 lakh–₹2.1 lakh for 3 kW and ₹4.8 lakh–₹6 lakh for 10 kW, before the PM Surya Ghar subsidy.

How much subsidy is available for rooftop solar?

Under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW, up to ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above. It is paid to your bank account after the net meter is installed.

Which size of solar system do I need?

Divide your average monthly units by about 120. A home using 360 units a month needs about 3 kW. Each kW needs about 100 sq ft of shadow-free roof.

How many units does 1kW solar generate per day?

About 3.3–4.5 units a day, or 100–138 units a month, depending on the city. 1kW generation by city.
