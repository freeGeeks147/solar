Words you will see

kW (kilowatt)
The size of a solar system. Bigger kW = more panels = more electricity.
Unit
One unit on your electricity bill (1 kWh). A fan running for a full day uses about 1.5 units.
Subsidy
Money the government pays back to you under PM Surya Ghar, after the system is installed.
On-grid
Solar connected to the electricity line, without batteries. Only on-grid systems get the subsidy.
Net meter
A two-way meter. It counts units you take from the line and units your panels send to it.
DCR panels
Panels made with Indian cells. Required for the subsidy; your installer will use them.

Common questions

How much do solar panels cost for a home in India in 2026?

A complete system costs about ₹60,000 to ₹80,000 for 1 kW, ₹1.65 lakh to ₹2.1 lakh for 3 kW and ₹4.8 lakh to ₹6 lakh for 10 kW. The government subsidy then pays you back up to ₹78,000.

How much subsidy does the government give?

Under PM Surya Ghar: ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and ₹78,000 for 3 kW or more. It is paid into your bank account after installation.

What size of solar do I need?

Look at your monthly bill. Roughly: up to ₹1,000 → 1 kW, ₹2,000 → 2 kW, ₹2,500–3,000 → 3 kW, ₹4,000–5,000 → 5 kW. Each kW needs about 100 sq ft of open roof.

How many units does 1kW solar make in a day?

About 4 units a day, a little more in sunny states. See your city.
