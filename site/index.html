Frequently asked questions

What is the electricity unit rate in Gujarat in 2026-27?

For homes on the state DISCOMs (UGVCL, MGVCL, DGVCL, PGVCL) the urban residential rate is ₹3.05/unit for the first 50 units, ₹3.50 for 51–100, ₹4.15 for 101–250 and ₹5.20 above 250 units a month, plus FPPPA and 15% duty. Torrent Power has its own rates for Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar and Surat.

Which DISCOM supplies my area?

Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar cities and Surat city are supplied by Torrent Power. North Gujarat is UGVCL, central Gujarat is MGVCL, south Gujarat is DGVCL, and Saurashtra and Kutch are PGVCL. The company name is printed at the top of your bill.

Did Gujarat electricity rates change in 2026-27?

GERC kept base energy and fixed charges unchanged for 2026-27. It raised the prepaid smart meter rebate to 3% and widened the 60 paise/unit smart meter discount window to 11:00-17:00.
