How your bill is calculated

  1. Energy charge: units are split across the slabs above, and each slab is charged at its own rate.
  2. Fixed charge: a monthly amount based on your sanctioned load.
  3. FPPPA: a per-unit fuel surcharge on every unit.
  4. Rebates: the prepaid rebate and ToU discount are subtracted if you have a smart meter.
  5. Electricity duty: a percentage of the total, levied by the state government.

Frequently asked questions

What is the DGVCL per unit rate in 2026-27?

For residential (RGP) consumers, DGVCL charges ₹3.05 per unit for the first 50 units a month, rising to ₹5.20 per unit above 250 units. FPPPA of about ₹2.30 per unit and 15% electricity duty are added on top, so the all-in rate is usually ₹6–9 per unit.

How much is the DGVCL bill for 200 units?

About ₹1,400 for 200 units in a month at 2 kW load: ₹743 energy charge, ₹15 fixed charge, ₹460 FPPPA and ₹183 electricity duty.

How much is the DGVCL bill for 300 units?

About ₹2,214 for 300 units in a month at 3 kW load. At that level each extra unit costs about ₹8.63 including FPPPA and duty, which is the rate rooftop solar saves you.

What is FPPPA on my bill?

FPPPA (Fuel and Power Purchase Price Adjustment) passes changes in fuel and power purchase costs to consumers. It is charged per unit on every unit, is revised each quarter, and is printed on your bill. Enter the current figure in the calculator for an exact match.

Does DGVCL bill monthly or every two months?

Gujarat state DISCOMs, including DGVCL, usually bill residential consumers every two months. The slab limits apply per month, so a 2-month bill doubles each slab. Choose the 2-month billing period in the calculator and enter the units from your bill.

How much can rooftop solar save on a DGVCL bill?

A home using 300 units a month would typically fit a 2 kW system. After the ₹60,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy it costs around ₹50,000, cuts the bill by about ₹1,858 a month and pays back in roughly 2.2 years. Check your own numbers.
