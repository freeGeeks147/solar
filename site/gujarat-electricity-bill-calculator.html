Common questions

What is the electricity unit rate in Gujarat in 2026-27?

For homes on UGVCL, MGVCL, DGVCL and PGVCL in cities: ₹3.05 a unit for the first 50 units, ₹3.50 for 51–100, ₹4.15 for 101–250 and ₹5.20 above 250 units a month, plus fuel surcharge and duty. Torrent Power has its own rates for Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar and Surat.

Which electricity company supplies my area?

Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar and Surat cities: Torrent Power. North Gujarat: UGVCL. Central Gujarat: MGVCL. South Gujarat: DGVCL. Saurashtra and Kutch: PGVCL. The name is printed at the top of your bill.

Did Gujarat electricity rates change in 2026-27?

The base rates stayed the same. The prepaid smart meter discount went up to 3%, and the smart meter daytime discount (60 paise a unit) now runs from 11 am to 5 pm.
