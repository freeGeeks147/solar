4kW Solar System Price in India 2026 (With Subsidy)

A 4kW on-grid rooftop system costs ₹2.15 lakh–₹2.7 lakh installed, or ₹1.37 lakh–₹1.92 lakh after the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy. It makes about 16 units a day.

What a 4kW system includes

  • Panels: 8 × 550 W DCR (made-in-India cells), needed for the subsidy
  • Inverter: 4 kW on-grid string inverter
  • Mounting structure: galvanised steel; elevated structures cost more
  • Wiring and protection: DC/AC cables, ACDB/DCDB, earthing, lightning arrester
  • Net meter: applied through your DISCOM; meter and testing fees are usually extra

What changes the price

  • Brand: Adani, Waaree, Tata Power Solar, Vikram, Premier and others differ by about 5–15% for the same size.
  • Panel type: TOPCon and bifacial panels cost a little more than Mono PERC but produce more per square foot.
  • Structure height and roof type (RCC, tin shed).
  • Battery: hybrid or off-grid systems with batteries cost much more and the PM Surya Ghar subsidy covers on-grid systems.

4kW generation and savings

At a typical site a 4kW system makes about 15.9 units a day, 483 a month and 5,800 a year. Valued at ₹7/unit for a home using 480 units a month, that saves about ₹3,254 a month and pays back the after-subsidy cost in about 4.1 years. City-wise 4kW generation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the price of a 4kW solar system in India in 2026?

A 4kW on-grid rooftop system with DCR panels typically costs ₹2.15 lakh–₹2.7 lakh installed. After the PM Surya Ghar subsidy of ₹78,000 you pay about ₹1.37 lakh–₹1.92 lakh.

What is the 4kW solar system price with subsidy?

About ₹1.37 lakh–₹1.92 lakh after the ₹78,000 central subsidy (the subsidy is capped at ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above). Some states add their own top-up on top of this.

How many units does a 4kW solar system generate per day?

About 13.2–18.1 units a day depending on the city, around 483 units a month and 5,800 a year at a typical site. See city-wise generation.

How many panels are needed for 4kW?

8 panels of 550 W (4.40 kW). With smaller 400–450 W panels you need 10–10.

How much roof area does 4kW solar need?

About 400 sq ft of shadow-free roof, roughly 37 square metres.

Is 4kW enough for my home?

A 4kW system covers a home using about 480 units a month. Check the units on your last few bills; size the system to your average use, because surplus units earn only ₹2–3 each from the DISCOM.

Can a 4kW solar system run an AC?

On-grid solar does not power the AC directly; it offsets your bill. 4kW makes about 16 units a day, enough to cover a 1.5-ton inverter AC for about 12 hours of daily use.
