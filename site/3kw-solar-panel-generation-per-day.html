How to check your own system

  1. Open your inverter app or display and note the daily units ("Today" or "E-Day").
  2. Compare a sunny week's average with the units per day for your city above.
  3. Below 80% for weeks at a time: clean the panels, look for new shade, and ask your installer to check the inverter for trips.

Frequently asked questions

How many units does a 3kW solar panel generate per day?

9.9–13.6 units a day on average, depending on the city. That is about 4 units per kW in cloudier places such as Kolkata and Guwahati and about 4.5 units per kW in Rajasthan and Gujarat.

How many units does a 3kW solar system generate per month?

About 300–413 units a month on average. Output is highest in March to May and lowest in the monsoon months.

How many units does 3kW solar produce in a year?

About 3,600–4,950 units a year, falling by about 0.5% a year as panels age.

Why is my 3kW system generating less than expected?

Common reasons are shade from a water tank, parapet or tree, dust on panels (clean every 2–3 weeks in dry months), the monsoon season, the inverter tripping on high grid voltage, and wrong panel direction. A system making less than 80% of the figures here for a whole month is worth checking with your installer.

How much bill does 3kW solar save?

At ₹7/unit, 363 units a month is worth about ₹2,538 a month, as long as you use those units yourself. See 3kW price and payback.
