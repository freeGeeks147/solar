Frequently asked questions

How many units does a 3kW solar panel make per day?

About 12 units a day on average. Sunny places like Rajasthan and Gujarat get up to 13.6; cloudier places like Kolkata and Guwahati get about 9.9.

How many units does a 3kW solar system make per month?

About 300 to 413 units a month. Most in March to May, least in the rainy months.

How many units does 3kW solar make in a year?

About 3,600 to 4,950 units a year.

Why is my 3kW system making less than this?

The usual reasons: shade from a water tank, wall or tree; dusty panels (clean them every 2–3 weeks when it is dry); cloudy or rainy weather; or the inverter switching off. If you get less than 80% of these figures for a whole month, call your installer.

How much money does 3kW solar save?

About ₹2,538 a month at ₹7 per unit, if your home uses the units it makes. See 3kW price and savings.
