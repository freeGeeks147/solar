For information only

Bijli Bajao is an independent website. All calculators, prices, subsidy amounts, electricity rates, generation figures and savings shown here are estimates. They are worked out from information publicly available on the internet, such as electricity tariff orders, government scheme websites and solar installer price lists.

Not official, not endorsed

This website is not run by, connected to, or endorsed by the Government of India, any state government, the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy, any electricity regulatory commission, any electricity distribution company (such as UGVCL, MGVCL, DGVCL, PGVCL or Torrent Power), any solar installer or any solar brand. Brand and company names are used only to describe them.

Your actual figures can differ

  • Electricity rates, fuel surcharges and taxes change, sometimes every few months.
  • Solar prices vary by brand, installer, city and roof.
  • Solar output depends on weather, shade, dust and how the system is installed.
  • Government schemes and subsidy rules can change or end.

Before you decide

Please confirm rates with your electricity company, subsidy details on the official PM Surya Ghar portal, and prices with 2 or 3 installers. We are not responsible for any decision, loss or expense based on information from this website.
