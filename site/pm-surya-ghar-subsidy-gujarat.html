PM Surya Ghar Subsidy in Gujarat

How much central subsidy you get for rooftop solar, what you pay after it, and the steps to apply through your DISCOM.

Subsidy amount

  • ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW
  • ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW
  • Maximum ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW and above

The subsidy is for residential connections only and is paid into your bank account after the system is installed, inspected and net-metered.

How to apply

  1. Register on the national PM Surya Ghar portal with your DISCOM consumer number and mobile number.
  2. Apply for rooftop solar and wait for the DISCOM's feasibility approval.
  3. Choose a vendor registered with your DISCOM and get the system installed.
  4. Submit the installation details and apply for the net meter.
  5. After the DISCOM inspects and commissions the net meter, submit your bank details for the subsidy.
