Smart Meter Time-of-Use Discount in Gujarat

From 1 April 2026, smart-meter homes in Gujarat pay less for electricity used in the solar hours.

What changed in 2026-27

  • The ToU discount of 60 paise per unit now covers 11:00-17:00 (6 hours), up from 11:00–15:00.
  • It now applies to postpaid smart-meter consumers too, not only prepaid.
  • The prepaid smart meter rebate went up from 2% to 3% of energy charges.
  • No peak-hour surcharge is modelled for residential consumers in this calculator.

Who is eligible

RGP consumers with a smart meter (prepaid or postpaid). Ordinary electromechanical or non-smart meters cannot record time of use, so the discount does not apply.

How much it saves

Moving 100 units a month (washing machine, water heater, pump, EV charging) into 11:00-17:00 saves ₹60 plus duty, about ₹69 a month. Use the bill calculator with a smart meter selected to see your exact figure.
