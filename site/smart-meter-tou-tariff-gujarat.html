Smart Meter Discount in Gujarat

From 1 April 2026, smart-meter homes pay less for electricity used in the daytime.

Discount hours
11 am to 5 pm
Discount
60 paise a unit
Prepaid meter
3% off energy charge

What changed in 2026-27

  • The 60 paise discount now covers 11 am to 5 pm (6 hours), up from 11 am to 3 pm.
  • It now applies to smart meters on monthly bills too, not only prepaid ones.
  • The prepaid meter discount went up from 2% to 3%.

Who gets it

Homes with a smart meter. Ordinary meters cannot record the time of use, so the discount does not apply.

How much it saves

Moving 100 units a month (washing machine, water heater, pump, EV charging) into 11 am–5 pm saves about ₹69 a month including tax. Try the bill calculator.
