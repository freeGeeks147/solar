Other charges on your bill

  • Fuel surcharge (FPPPA): about ₹2.30 on every unit. Changes every three months.
  • Electricity duty: a state tax, about 15% in cities and 7.5% in villages.
  • Prepaid smart meter: 3% off the energy charge.
  • Smart meter daytime discount: 60 paise off every unit used between 11 am and 5 pm. How it works

Common questions

What is the PGVCL per unit rate in 2026-27?

For homes, PGVCL charges ₹3.05 per unit for the first 50 units a month, rising to ₹5.20 per unit at higher use. A fuel surcharge (FPPPA) of about ₹2.30 per unit and electricity duty are added, so the all-in rate is usually ₹6–9 per unit.

How much is the PGVCL bill for 200 units?

About ₹1,400 for 200 units in a month at 2 kW load.

How much is the PGVCL bill for 300 units?

About ₹2,214 for 300 units in a month at 3 kW load. At that level each extra unit costs about ₹8.63, which is what rooftop solar saves you per unit.

What is the fuel surcharge (FPPPA) on my bill?

It passes changes in fuel and power purchase costs to consumers. It is charged on every unit, changes every three months, and is printed on your bill. Enter the current figure in the calculator for an exact match.

Does PGVCL bill monthly or every two months?

PGVCL usually bills homes every two months. Choose the 2-month billing period in the calculator and enter the units from your bill.

How much can rooftop solar save on a PGVCL bill?

A home using 300 units a month would typically fit a 2 kW system. After the ₹60,000 subsidy it costs about ₹50,000, cuts the bill by about ₹1,858 a month and pays back in about 2.2 years. Check your own numbers.
