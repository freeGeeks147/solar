How to check your own system

  1. Open your inverter app or display and note the daily units ("Today" or "E-Day").
  2. Compare a sunny week's average with the units per day for your city above.
  3. Below 80% for weeks at a time: clean the panels, look for new shade, and ask your installer to check the inverter for trips.

Frequently asked questions

How many units does a 10kW solar panel generate per day?

32.9–45.2 units a day on average, depending on the city. That is about 4 units per kW in cloudier places such as Kolkata and Guwahati and about 4.5 units per kW in Rajasthan and Gujarat.

How many units does a 10kW solar system generate per month?

About 1000–1375 units a month on average. Output is highest in March to May and lowest in the monsoon months.

How many units does 10kW solar produce in a year?

About 12,000–16,500 units a year, falling by about 0.5% a year as panels age.

Why is my 10kW system generating less than expected?

Common reasons are shade from a water tank, parapet or tree, dust on panels (clean every 2–3 weeks in dry months), the monsoon season, the inverter tripping on high grid voltage, and wrong panel direction. A system making less than 80% of the figures here for a whole month is worth checking with your installer.

How much bill does 10kW solar save?

At ₹7/unit, 1208 units a month is worth about ₹8,458 a month, as long as you use those units yourself. See 10kW price and payback.
