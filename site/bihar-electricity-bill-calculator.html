Other charges on your bill

  • Fuel surcharge (FPPAS): about ₹0.00 a unit in our estimate; it changes often, so copy it from your bill.
  • Electricity duty: a state tax, taken as 6%.
  • Units above 125 are charged at the rate after the state government's subsidy: about Rs 5.52 a unit in towns and Rs 2.45 in villages. Without subsidy the BERC rates are higher (Rs 7.42 and up).
  • The fixed charge is taken as Rs 80 per kW a month in towns and Rs 40 per kW in villages, and is always included (it is not clear whether it is waived within the free units).

Common questions

What is the electricity rate per unit in Bihar in 2026-27?

Homes get the first 125 units a month free. Above that, our estimate is about ₹5.52 a unit in towns after the state subsidy, plus a fixed charge and 6% duty.

How much is the electricity bill for 200 units in Bihar?

About ₹693 for 200 units in a month in a town (our estimate, with typical charges).

How much is the electricity bill for 300 units in Bihar?

About ₹1,278 a month in a town. At that level each extra unit costs about ₹5.85, which is roughly what rooftop solar saves per unit.

What is Fuel surcharge (FPPAS) on my bill?

It passes changes in fuel and power purchase costs on to consumers, and it changes often. It is printed on your bill; type it into the calculator for an exact match.

Is the bill monthly in Bihar?

Yes, home bills are usually monthly. If yours covers two months, choose the 2-month billing period.
