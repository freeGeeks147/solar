How to apply

  • Register on the PM Surya Ghar portal with your electricity consumer number.
  • Choose a registered vendor, get 2–3 quotes with DCR (made-in-India) panels, and install.
  • After the net meter is fitted and the system is inspected, the subsidy is paid into your bank account.

Common questions

How much does rooftop solar cost in Madhya Pradesh after subsidy?

A 3 kW system typically costs ₹1.65 lakh to ₹2.1 lakh. PM Surya Ghar takes off ₹78,000.

How much electricity does 1 kW solar make in Madhya Pradesh?

About 4.2 units a day on average (1,520 a year) in Indore, less in the monsoon and more in March to May.

How long does solar take to pay back in Madhya Pradesh?

For a home in Indore using 300 units a month, a 2.0 kW system costs about ₹50,000 after subsidy, saves about ₹2,398 a month and pays back in about 1.7 years (our estimate).

What happens to extra solar units?

With net metering, units you do not use go to the grid and are set off against units you take. Surplus left over is paid at a low rate, about ₹3.00 a unit in our estimate, so size the system to your own use.
