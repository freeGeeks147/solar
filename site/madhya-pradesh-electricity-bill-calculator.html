Other charges on your bill

  • Fuel surcharge (FPPAS): about ₹0.30 a unit in our estimate; it changes often, so copy it from your bill.
  • Electricity duty: a state tax, taken as 9%.
  • Energy rates are the same in cities and villages; the fixed charge is lower in villages.
  • Fixed charge: a flat amount up to 150 units a month; above that it is charged for every 15 units (each counted as 0.1 kW of load).
  • FPPAS is a monthly percentage of the bill (5.36% was reported for April 2026); it is shown here as about Rs 0.30 a unit.
  • The calculator shows the full tariff, before the Gruh Jyoti subsidy.

Gruh Jyoti (Atal Gruh Jyoti) scheme: if you use up to 150 units in a month, you pay only Rs 100 for the first 100 units.

Common questions

What is the electricity rate per unit in Madhya Pradesh in 2026-27?

For homes, our estimate is ₹4.71 a unit for the first 50 units a month, rising to ₹7.24 at higher use, plus a fixed charge, Fuel surcharge (FPPAS) and 9% electricity duty. All in, 300 units cost about ₹9.27 a unit.

How much is the electricity bill for 200 units in Madhya Pradesh?

About ₹1,782 for 200 units in a month in a town (our estimate, with typical charges).

How much is the electricity bill for 300 units in Madhya Pradesh?

About ₹2,780 a month in a town. At that level each extra unit costs about ₹40.92, which is roughly what rooftop solar saves per unit.

What is Fuel surcharge (FPPAS) on my bill?

It passes changes in fuel and power purchase costs on to consumers, and it changes often. It is printed on your bill; type it into the calculator for an exact match.

Is the bill monthly in Madhya Pradesh?

Yes, home bills are usually monthly. If yours covers two months, choose the 2-month billing period.
