Other charges on your bill

  • Fuel adjustment (FAC): about ₹0.00 a unit in our estimate; it changes often, so copy it from your bill.
  • Electricity duty: a state tax, taken as 16%.
  • Slab rates include the wheeling charge (about Rs 1.20 a unit), which MSEDCL bills on every unit.
  • MERC reviewed its March 2025 order and revised it in June 2025; the FY 2026-27 figures here follow the published summary and may differ slightly from your bill.
  • The small per-unit 'tax on sale of electricity' is not included.
  • Mumbai city and suburbs are served by BEST, Adani Electricity and Tata Power, which have different rates; this calculator is for MSEDCL (Mahavitaran).

SMART scheme: extra state subsidy for rooftop solar for BPL and low-income homes using under 100 units a month (reported as Rs 17,500 per kW for BPL and Rs 10,000 per kW for other eligible homes, on top of PM Surya Ghar). Check current status with MSEDCL.

Common questions

What is the electricity rate per unit in Maharashtra in 2026-27?

For homes, our estimate is ₹5.52 a unit for the first 100 units a month, rising to ₹15.17 at higher use, plus a fixed charge, Fuel adjustment (FAC) and 16% electricity duty. All in, 300 units cost about ₹10.83 a unit.

How much is the electricity bill for 200 units in Maharashtra?

About ₹2,021 for 200 units in a month in a town (our estimate, with typical charges).

How much is the electricity bill for 300 units in Maharashtra?

About ₹3,250 a month in a town. At that level each extra unit costs about ₹15.90, which is roughly what rooftop solar saves per unit.

What is Fuel adjustment (FAC) on my bill?

It passes changes in fuel and power purchase costs on to consumers, and it changes often. It is printed on your bill; type it into the calculator for an exact match.

Is the bill monthly in Maharashtra?

Yes, home bills are usually monthly. If yours covers two months, choose the 2-month billing period.
