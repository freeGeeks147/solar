Latest updates

  • 22 October 2026 Reported

    Gujarat adds its own subsidy for small rooftop systems

    Gujarat will top up the PM Surya Ghar subsidy for homes installing up to 2.18 kW from 22 October 2026, so the household pays only 10% of the benchmark cost (5% for SC/ST families). Systems above 2.18 kW do not get the state top-up. The exact rupee amounts depend on the benchmark cost in the state order, so check with your DISCOM.

    Sources: Mercom India · Energetica India

  • 4 August 2026 Reported

    PM Surya Ghar crosses 50 lakh rooftop installations

    Over 50 lakh homes now have rooftop solar under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, with about 14.8 GW installed. July 2026 added a record 5.06 lakh homes in a single month.

    Sources: ANI · Outlook Business

  • 25 March 2026 Official

    New Gujarat electricity tariffs for 2026-27

    The Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission issued tariff orders for FY 2026-27 for the state DISCOMs (UGVCL, MGVCL, DGVCL, PGVCL) and Torrent Power, effective from 1 April 2026. The bill calculators on this site use these orders.

    Sources: GERC · SolarQuarter

  • 22 September 2025 Reported

    GST on solar panels cut from 12% to 5%

    Following the 56th GST Council meeting, GST on solar power devices, solar panels and cells and other renewable energy devices fell from 12% to 5% from 22 September 2025, making rooftop systems a little cheaper.

    Sources: Upstox News · Energetica India

  • 29 February 2024 Official

    Cabinet approves PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana

    The Union Cabinet approved the scheme with an outlay of ₹75,021 crore to put rooftop solar on 1 crore homes, with up to 300 units of free electricity a month. The subsidy is ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and ₹78,000 for 3 kW or more.

    Sources: PM India · Amar Ujala
