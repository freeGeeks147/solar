How your bill is calculated

  1. Energy charge: units are split across the slabs above, and each slab is charged at its own rate.
  2. Fixed charge: a monthly amount based on your sanctioned load or supply phase.
  3. FPPPA: a per-unit fuel surcharge on every unit.
  4. Rebates: the prepaid rebate and ToU discount are subtracted if you have a smart meter.
  5. Electricity duty: a percentage of the total, levied by the state government.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Torrent Power Surat per unit rate in 2026-27?

For residential (RGP) consumers, Torrent Power Surat charges ₹3.20 per unit for the first 50 units a month, rising to ₹5.05 per unit above 250 units. FPPPA of about ₹2.30 per unit and 15% electricity duty are added on top, so the all-in rate is usually ₹6–9 per unit.

How much is the Torrent Power Surat bill for 200 units?

About ₹1,440 for 200 units in a month at 2 kW load: ₹768 energy charge, ₹25 fixed charge, ₹460 FPPPA and ₹188 electricity duty.

How much is the Torrent Power Surat bill for 300 units?

About ₹2,240 for 300 units in a month at 3 kW load. At that level each extra unit costs about ₹8.45 including FPPPA and duty, which is the rate rooftop solar saves you.

What is FPPPA on my bill?

FPPPA (Fuel and Power Purchase Price Adjustment) passes changes in fuel and power purchase costs to consumers. It is charged per unit on every unit, is revised each quarter, and is printed on your bill. Enter the current figure in the calculator for an exact match.

Does Torrent Power Surat bill monthly or every two months?

Torrent Power residential bills are generally monthly. If your bill covers two months, choose the 2-month billing period: the slab limits are doubled.

How much can rooftop solar save on a Torrent Power Surat bill?

A home using 300 units a month would typically fit a 2 kW system. After the ₹60,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy it costs around ₹50,000, cuts the bill by about ₹1,875 a month and pays back in roughly 2.2 years. Check your own numbers.
