Common questions

What is the Torrent Power Surat per unit rate in 2026-27?

For homes, Torrent Power Surat charges ₹3.20 per unit for the first 50 units a month, rising to ₹5.05 per unit at higher use. A fuel surcharge (FPPPA) of about ₹2.30 per unit and electricity duty are added, so the all-in rate is usually ₹6–9 per unit.

How much is the Torrent Power Surat bill for 200 units?

About ₹1,440 for 200 units in a month at 2 kW load.

How much is the Torrent Power Surat bill for 300 units?

About ₹2,240 for 300 units in a month at 3 kW load. At that level each extra unit costs about ₹8.45, which is what rooftop solar saves you per unit.

What is the fuel surcharge (FPPPA) on my bill?

It passes changes in fuel and power purchase costs to consumers. It is charged on every unit, changes every three months, and is printed on your bill. Enter the current figure in the calculator for an exact match.

Does Torrent Power Surat bill monthly or every two months?

Torrent Power home bills are generally monthly. If your bill covers two months, choose the 2-month billing period.

How much can rooftop solar save on a Torrent Power Surat bill?

A home using 300 units a month would typically fit a 2 kW system. After the ₹60,000 subsidy it costs about ₹50,000, cuts the bill by about ₹1,875 a month and pays back in about 2.2 years. Check your own numbers.
