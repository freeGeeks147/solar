What you get for the price

  • 19 solar panels (550 watts each, made in India)
  • A 10 kW inverter, the box that turns solar power into home electricity
  • A steel stand to hold the panels on your roof
  • Wiring, safety switches and earthing
  • Installation. The two-way meter (net meter) comes from your electricity company, usually for a small fee.

Why prices differ

  • Brand: Adani, Waaree, Tata Power Solar and others differ by about 5–15%.
  • Stand height: a raised stand (so you can use the roof below) costs more.
  • Battery: a system with batteries costs much more, and does not get the subsidy.

How solar saves money: two examples

Each solar unit your home uses replaces a unit you would buy from the electricity company. Extra units go to the grid and earn only a little. Example at ₹7 a unit:

Words you will see

kW (kilowatt)
The size of a solar system. Bigger kW = more panels = more electricity.
Unit
One unit on your electricity bill (1 kWh). A fan running for a full day uses about 1.5 units.
Subsidy
Money the government pays back to you under PM Surya Ghar, after the system is installed.
On-grid
Solar connected to the electricity line, without batteries. Only on-grid systems get the subsidy.
Net meter
A two-way meter. It counts units you take from the line and units your panels send to it.
DCR panels
Panels made with Indian cells. Required for the subsidy; your installer will use them.

Common questions

What is the price of a 10kW solar system in India in 2026?

A complete 10kW rooftop system costs ₹4.8 lakh to ₹6 lakh, installed. After the government subsidy of ₹78,000, you pay ₹4.02 lakh to ₹5.22 lakh.

What is the 10kW solar system price with subsidy?

₹4.02 lakh to ₹5.22 lakh. The government pays ₹78,000 back into your bank account after installation (₹78,000 is the maximum, for 3 kW and above).

How many units does a 10kW solar system make per day?

About 40 units a day (33 to 45 depending on your city). That is about 1208 units a month. See your city.

How many panels are needed for 10kW?

19 panels of 550 watts each. If your installer uses smaller panels, you need a few more.

How much roof space does 10kW solar need?

About 1000 sq ft of open roof with no shade.

Is 10kW enough for my home?

10kW suits a home with a monthly bill of about ₹8,500 (1210 units). If your bill is much smaller, choose a smaller system: extra units sold to the grid earn very little.

Can a 10kW solar system run an AC?

Yes, in effect. 10kW makes about 40 units a day, enough for a 1.5-ton inverter AC for about 31 hours a day. The AC runs as normal and your bill goes down.
