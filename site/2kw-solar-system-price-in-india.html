What you get for the price

  • 4 solar panels (550 watts each, made in India)
  • A 2 kW inverter, the box that turns solar power into home electricity
  • A steel stand to hold the panels on your roof
  • Wiring, safety switches and earthing
  • Installation. The new two-way meter (net meter) comes from your electricity company, usually for a small fee.

Why prices differ

  • Brand: Adani, Waaree, Tata Power Solar and others differ by about 5–15%.
  • Stand height: a raised stand (so you can use the roof below) costs more.
  • Battery: a system with batteries costs much more, and does not get the subsidy.

Words you will see

kW (kilowatt)
The size of a solar system. Bigger kW = more panels = more electricity.
Unit
One unit on your electricity bill (1 kWh). A fan running for a full day uses about 1.5 units.
Subsidy
Money the government pays back to you under PM Surya Ghar, after the system is installed.
On-grid
Solar connected to the electricity line, without batteries. Only on-grid systems get the subsidy.
Net meter
A two-way meter. It counts units you take from the line and units your panels send to it.
DCR panels
Panels made with Indian cells. Required for the subsidy; your installer will use them.

Frequently asked questions

What is the price of a 2kW solar system in India in 2026?

A complete 2kW rooftop system costs ₹1.2 lakh to ₹1.5 lakh, installed. After the government subsidy of ₹60,000, you pay ₹60,000 to ₹90,000.

What is the 2kW solar system price with subsidy?

₹60,000 to ₹90,000. The government pays ₹60,000 back into your bank account after installation.

How many units does a 2kW solar system make per day?

About 8 units a day (7 to 9 depending on your city). That is about 242 units a month. See your city.

How many panels are needed for 2kW?

4 panels of 550 watts each. If your installer uses smaller panels, you need a few more.

How much roof space does 2kW solar need?

About 200 sq ft of open roof with no shade, roughly a 12 × 16 ft area.

Is 2kW enough for my home?

2kW suits a home with a monthly bill of about ₹1,700 (240 units). If your bill is much smaller, choose a smaller system: extra units sold to the grid earn very little.

Can a 2kW solar system run an AC?

Yes, in effect. 2kW makes about 8 units a day, enough for a 1.5-ton inverter AC for about 6 hours a day. The panels feed your home and the grid; the AC runs as normal and your bill goes down.
